Your location & privacy

GPS is requested when you use a location action or start navigation. Navigation tracking stops when you end it or leave the page.

Place searches, route endpoints and nearby-help coordinates go through this server to Geoapify. Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap. GPS updates during navigation stay in your browser.

Contacts stay in this browser’s local storage. Reports and review history are stored on the server. Clearing browser cookies removes access to your report list; delete reports first if you want to remove them.

Shared-device users can see browser-stored contacts. Sharing uses your device’s share sheet or a copyable message; no messages are sent automatically.
